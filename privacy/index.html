Privacy

What you write here stays with you

This site doesn't collect, store, or send anything you type. The tools are here for you to use on your own, keep on your own device, and share only if and how you choose.

The short version

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or cookies.
  • No third-party scripts, embeds, or fonts. Everything on the page is served from this site.
  • Nothing you type in a tool is sent to me or anyone else.

When a tool saves your progress

A few longer tools, like Values Clarification, save your answers in your browser so you can stop and come back. That copy lives only on the device you're using. I can't see it, and it never leaves your device.

On a shared or public computer, use Erase my answers when you're done. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.

Keeping and sharing your results

You decide what happens to what you've written. You can print it, save it as a PDF, or copy the text. If you're working with a therapist, you might bring it to a session, show it on your screen, or email it yourself if the two of you have agreed to use email. The site itself never sends it anywhere.

Contacting me and sharing tools

The contact form and the "Share a Tool with a Client" form don't send anything through this site. They open your own email app with a message ready for you to review and send. Please keep personal health details out of email; we can talk about those in session.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operations. These logs aren't shared with me, and I don't use them.

Counseling records

If you're a counseling client, your records are kept securely by the practice where you receive care, never on this site.

Not a crisis service

These tools are for reflection and aren't a substitute for care. If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline in the US), or call 911.
