Privacy

What you write here stays with you

This site doesn't collect, store, or send anything you type. My tools now live on Beyond the Room, with the same promise.

The short version

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or cookies.
  • No third-party scripts, embeds, or fonts. Everything on the page is served from this site.
  • Nothing you type on this site is sent anywhere except through your own email app, when you choose to.

Where the tools live now

My interactive tools, the Therapy Resources library, and the For Therapists toolkit now live on Beyond the Room, which follows the same privacy rules. Its privacy page explains how tools save progress on your device and how to erase it.

Contacting me

The contact form doesn't send anything through this site. They open your own email app with a message ready for you to review and send. Please keep personal health details out of email; we can talk about those in session.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operations. These logs aren't shared with me, and I don't use them.

Counseling records

If you're a counseling client, your records are kept securely by the practice where you receive care, never on this site.

Not a crisis service

My tools are for reflection and aren't a substitute for care. If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline in the US), or call 911.

Questions about privacy? Email wooheekwak@outlook.com.
