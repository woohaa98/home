My Inner Landscape

Write, draw, or use single words. Start anywhere, and leave spaces blank if you like. Roots are where you come from; the top is who you're becoming.

Weather What's weighing on me worries, pressures, inner critic
New growth Who I'm becoming hopes, goals, intentions
Leaves Who sustains me people, places, communities
Branches How I spend my life roles, work, commitments, interests
Trunk What I stand for values and strengths
Falling leaves What I'm letting go of
Roots Where I come from family, culture, early lessons, beliefs I inherited
Soil What I need to thrive rest, safety, belonging, meaning, play
