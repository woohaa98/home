Parts of Me

A gentle reflection for getting to know one part of you: the one that pushes, protects, numbs, or doubts. Not to fix it, but to understand what it's been trying to do for you.

    Grounding pause

    Feel your feet on the floor. Let your breath slow down, a little longer on the out-breath. Look around and name three things you can see. You can stop here at any time.
