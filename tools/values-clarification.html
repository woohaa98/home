Values Clarification Exercise

For anyone figuring out what's next: choosing a path before or during college, starting out, or feeling stuck in a career. Discover what matters most to you, put it in your own words, and see which kinds of work might fit. Takes about 15 to 20 minutes, and you can stop and come back.

🌿
Shared by your therapist, Elise Kwak

Take your time; your progress saves on this device so you can come back. At the end, you'll save a summary to bring to your next session. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.

    Where are you right now?

    This shapes the reflection questions you'll get at the end. Choose what fits best.

    Tip: on a phone, you can tap the microphone on your keyboard to speak instead of type.

    Which values speak to you?

    Choose every value that feels important to how you want to live and work. Go with your gut; you'll narrow down next. Aim for about 8 to 15.

    If you could keep only five…

    Which values would you hold on to, even when life gets busy or choices get hard? It's okay if this feels difficult. That's part of the clarity.

    Put them in order

    Use the arrows to move the value that matters most to the top. When two values compete, which would you choose?

      Make them yours

      The same word can mean very different things to different people. Describe each value in your own words, then notice how fully you're living it right now.
