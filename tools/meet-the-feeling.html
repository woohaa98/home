Meet the Feeling

Name what you feel, find where it lives in your body, ease that place with a short guided exercise, and let the feeling pass in its own time. Based on RAIN: Recognize, Allow, Investigate, Nurture. About 5 to 8 minutes.

Your answers stay on this device. Nothing is saved, tracked, or sent anywhere.

Living with OCD or panic? Soothing an anxious feeling can sometimes keep that cycle going. Ride the Wave is built for urges and compulsions.
