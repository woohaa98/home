Untangle the Pattern

Putting off homework because you're afraid it won't be good enough. Skipping a party because you're sure you won't measure up. This exercise helps you see how one rigid thought drives a feeling and a habit, and how to loosen it, one small step at a time. Mostly tapping; about 10 minutes.

    Take a breath

    Thoughts can feel very convincing when we're stressed. Breathe out slowly, feel your feet on the floor, and come back when you're ready. You can stop at any time.
