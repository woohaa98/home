RAIN Micropractice

A five-minute practice for meeting a hard feeling with kindness instead of fighting it. Four gentle steps: Recognize, Allow, Investigate, Nurture.

About RAIN

RAIN was first described by meditation teacher Michele McDonald and later adapted by psychologist and teacher Tara Brach, whose version ends with Nurture. It's widely used in mindfulness and self-compassion practice as a way to pause, make room for a feeling, and respond to yourself with care.

There's no right way to do it. You can write, or simply notice. If a step feels like too much, skip it, slow down, or stop.

A self-reflection practice, not a substitute for professional mental health care.
