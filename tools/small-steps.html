Small Steps

When mood is low, we tend to do less, and doing less makes mood lower. This planner helps you choose a few small, doable activities for the week, check them off, and notice how your mood responds. You don't have to feel motivated first. About 5 minutes to plan.

Your plan saves on this device so you can check things off during the week. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.
