Ride the Wave

When anxiety or panic hits, the urge to do something to make it stop can feel urgent: get air, wash, check, ask, escape. This practice helps you notice the whole cycle and stay with the feeling a little longer, so your brain can learn you can handle it.

    Slow it down

    Breathe in gently through your nose, then out through your mouth a little longer, like cooling soup. Feel where your body is supported. Name three things you can see. You don't have to make the feeling stop; just let your breath be slow.

    An educational, self-guided practice informed by exposure and response prevention (ERP) and mindfulness. It is not therapy and not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. For OCD or panic disorder, ERP and CBT work best with a trained therapist.
