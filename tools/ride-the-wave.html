Ride the Wave

For OCD, panic, and urges that feel like they must be obeyed: checking, washing, escaping, getting air, asking for reassurance. This practice isn't about calming down or proving the thought wrong. It helps you notice the urge and let it rise and fall without acting on it, so your brain learns you can handle discomfort and uncertainty.

    Slow it down

    Breathe in gently through your nose, then out through your mouth a little longer, like cooling soup. Feel where your body is supported. Name three things you can see. You don't have to make the feeling stop; just let your breath be slow.
